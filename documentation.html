🐶 Sam the Virtual Dog

Backstory

Sam is a Border Collie puppy who you got since he was 3 months. He's smart, expressive, and a little dramatic if you ignore him for too long, he'll let you know about it. Sam has a big personality packed into a small, fluffy body. He loves walks, treats, cuddles, and playtime. He hates being scolded, going hungry, and being left alone. Your job is to keep Sam happy, healthy, and entertained — forever. There's no winning, there's no ending. Sam is just your dog now.

Tips for Keeping Sam Happy

Watch out for these danger chains:
Hungry → (ignored) → Sick → very hard to fix without medicine
Sad → (ignored) → Sick → same problem
Bored → (ignored) → Angry → be careful, angry Buddy is hard to calm
